HViConnect Study Tracker

Privacy Policy

Overview

HViConnect Study Tracker does not collect, store, or share any personal information. This policy explains what that means for you.

HViConnect Study Tracker is published by Tulane University Heart and Vascular Institute for use by research staff. This policy applies to the app on iPhone and iPad.

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Information we collect

We do not collect any information from or about you. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Account credentials or user IDs
  • Health, medical, or research participant information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage data, analytics, or crash reports
  • Contacts, photos, files, or other content on your device

Information you enter

The app does not save anything you type or select. Nothing you enter is stored on your device after you close the app, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Please do not enter protected health information or other identifiable research participant data into the app.

How information is used and shared

Because we collect no information, we have nothing to use, share, or sell.

  • We do not sell or rent any data.
  • We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, sponsors, or other third parties.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not show advertising in the app.

Data storage, retention, and security

The app stores no user data, on your device or on any server. Because nothing is kept, there is nothing to retain, transfer, or delete, and no data can be exposed in a breach.

If you uninstall the app, nothing about you remains with us.

Third parties, permissions, and children

Third-party services. The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. Apple may collect information when you download or update apps from the App Store; that is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Device permissions. The app does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or health data.

Children. The app is intended for adult research staff and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If a future version of the app collects or stores any information, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the effective date above.
